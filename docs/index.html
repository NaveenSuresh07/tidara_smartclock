Design decisions

  • Alarms tied to sunrise and target sleep duration The sunrise arc dial visually connects wake-up times to local dawn alongside a target sleep duration card (defaulting to 7h 45m), removing mental math when planning morning alarms and evening wind-down.
  • Legibility first (WCAG 2.1 AA) with high-contrast support Frosted surfaces are benchmarked to keep primary and secondary text readable in both light and dark modes, with a dedicated High-Contrast toggle that replaces translucency with solid surfaces.
  • Interactive 3D globe and calmer focus sand timer A live day/night globe lets you see at a glance whether a contact is in daylight or asleep, while the sand hourglass pairs visual progress with practical 12m, 24m, 25m, 48m, 90m, and custom intervals.
